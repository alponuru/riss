Approach

03 / Method

Look closely.
Find the structure.
Make it visible.

Every project begins with context: what exists, what matters and what needs to be communicated. Research and observation help identify the relationships that give a project its character.

From there, I develop visual systems, images and spatial narratives that make those relationships legible — with attention to form, material, function and experience.

About RISS

04 / Practice

Across
disciplines.
With intent.

RISS is an independent design practice based in Trondheim, Norway, working with clients and collaborators locally and internationally.

The practice brings together graphic design, visual research and spatial thinking. Its work moves between image, information and environment — connecting conceptual exploration with clear, purposeful communication.

RISS is open to selected collaborations, freelance commissions and interdisciplinary projects.
